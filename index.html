How to play

  1. 1 creature per colorEvery colored patch gets exactly one.
  2. No creatures can touchNot side by side, and not even corner to corner.
  3. 1 creature per row and columnEvery row and every column gets exactly one.

Tap a square to mark ✕ where a creature can't go (tap again to clear). Double-tap to place a creature. Drag to mark lots of squares at once. A wrong guess costs a ♥ — lose all three and the board starts over.
