How to play

  • Place one weirdling in every row.
  • …and one in every column.
  • …and one in every colored patch.
  • Weirdlings are shy: they can't touch, not even corner to corner.

Tap a square to mark it ✕ when you know a weirdling can't go there. Tap again to place one. Drag across squares to mark lots at once. A wrong guess costs a ♥ — lose all three and you start the level over.
